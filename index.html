Bento Box In Comics

Comics, Manga, and Manhwa: different uses of the bento box

An insight on how the bento box is used to push and elevate stories from around the world.

An Early Example: Histoire de Mr. Vieux Bois

Rodolphe Töpffer's Histoire de Mr. Vieux Bois is one of the earliest known examples of a modern comic. The story was originally published in 1837 and was a short series of comics about a romance.

The main character is shown making attempts to get a girls attention and each attempt is seperated by a frame with a snipet of text. Before this point, there doesn't seem to be any story that specifically uses comic paneling like this to show a story.

the image to the left is an illustration that came from an adaption of Töpffer's book called The Adventures of Mr. Obadiah Oldbuck, which is typically regarded as the first comic printed in America.

For other mediums like Manga and Manhwa, the earliest use of the bento box is found after the second world war so they are not regarded as early to the form.

Comics, Manga, and Manhwa

These three forms of story telling are mainly seperated by country, not nessacarily much in style.

Comics / Spider-Man

Amazing Fantasy #15

Amazing Fantasy number 15 was the first comic featuring Spider-Man. Created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko, This comic marks itself as one of the most influential comics ever written.

Manga

One Piece

One Piece, written by Eiichiro Oda, is the single best selling Manga and Comic in history. It has shaped many of the stories to come after it and has run for an impressive 27 years as of 2026.

Manhwa

Omniscient Reader

Omniscient Reader is a famous Manhwa and the most recent release of the three examples. Although still a comparatively young work, it perfectly showcases the ways Manhwa have pushed the medium even further.

Why call it a bento box?

The Specific name only started being used in modern times but refers to the way in which a typical bento lunch is divided into rectangular compartments, much like comic panels.

Big moment means big panel.

If an artist wants a moment to stand out, it will often take up more space.

In passing

smaller moments require less focus and space.

A pause

The gap between panels is called a gutter and it allows for more visual clarity.

Keep moving

Panel order leads the reader through the story, even when the sizes change.

Stretching the form

The Ways they vary

While in the modern day, the different comic art forms take plently of inspiration from eachother, some of the ways in which they differ include: Manhwa using a scroll format where the page doesn't end. Manga having spreads and two page formatting. Or how both comics and manga often break the form completely in order to convey power or humor.

The importance of formatting

One of the most important aspects of the bento box is framing. for a page to interest a reader there needs to be strong use of space; should a big moment be given a majority of the page? should a fast movement be given a long panel? There are many ways you can use the form to benefit the story.

Dos and don'ts

Do: guide the reader

When creating a page it's important to be able to recognize where the readers eyes will naturally look. Where do you want to draw them first? how will you keep them from reading dialogue out of order? Every detail is crucial when planning your boxes.

Don't: fill every gap

A full page isn't always a good thing! An overabundance of panels will overwelm a reader and could even cause them to lose interest. Finding the line between your details and space is the most important thing.
